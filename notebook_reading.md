## 7. Reading it

In the order of the decision table.

1. **Gate** — `fixed` recipe hit. If the genome tracks the recipe on its own, nothing below
   is about within-life learning.
2. **`plastic (W2)` − `fixed`**, then **`plastic (W2)` − `scrambled`**. The second is the one
   that matters: `scrambled` has the same plasticity, the same H magnitudes and the same power
   to override the innate scaffold, and no information in the modulator.
3. **`probe_adv` (recipe)** — within-agent, so it cannot be a selection or composition effect.
   Read it together with **attempts/1k**: a hit rate bought by attempting less is abstention,
   not knowledge.
4. **`probe_adv` (food) and safe_rate** — the positive control. If the food effect is there and
   the recipe effect is not, the learner works in this world and the failure is specific to the
   conjunctive, delayed-credit fact.
5. **`fixed + B (ceiling)`** — if exact pair credit does not beat chance either, the world does
   not pay for the recipe and no condition can be read.
6. **The no-flip pair**, as a difference of differences.
7. **`bridge_steps` and `lam2^gap`** — the feasibility number. If what is left of the attempt's
   trace when the nut arrives is ~0, a null is a statement about the trace length, not about
   the rule.

## 8. Reading it

**Rows 1a and 2 are stop conditions.**

1. **Row 1a** — phase 1 must be v3.1. If not, nothing below is readable.
2. **Row 1b** — `fixed` must not hold the *conjunction*. A type-blind 0.5 is allowed; read the
   per-type split to tell them apart.
3. **Row 2** — food learning in the first mapping era, prepared meals per life ≥ 3 in `fixed`,
   P(prep | on food) against the null. Then **prep share by era** as the diagnostic.
4. **Row 3** — the abstention line first, then the two result lines, then **the per-type split**:
   a conjunction is *both* types above 0.5, not one at 1.0 and one at 0. `probe_adv` (prep) is
   within-agent and is the line that separates learning from survivorship — the pre-checks had
   `scrambled` at 0.722 on hit rate but only 0.342 on the probe, against plastic's 5.624.
5. **Row 5** — a null here, with rows 1–2 clean, is the first earned statement about the learner's
   limit: no approach behaviour required, credit immediate and two-sided, opportunity on every meal.
